What the farm does

  • Posts 24/7 on schedule

    Every page has its own posting plan. Some queue the night before, some fire just in time. Nobody has to press publish.

  • Writes its own captions

    Captions and hashtags come from an AI writer tuned per page, so each one keeps its own voice instead of sounding copied.

  • Edits photos automatically

    Cropping, framing, text overlays. Each image is prepared by the pipeline, so posts go out clean without anyone opening an editor.

  • Chases what's trending

    The farm scrapes news and trend sources all day, then queues posts about what people are already talking about while it's still hot.

  • Runs many pages at once

    Ten pages live on one server with one brain. Each keeps its own niche, voice and calendar, and none of them wait on a human.

  • Watches itself

    A watchdog checks the system every few minutes. If a job dies it gets restarted, and my phone gets a message about what happened.
